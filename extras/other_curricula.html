Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue Rhetoric independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

  • Silva Rhetoricae: The Forest of Rhetoric (Comprehensive online reference for classical rhetoric terms and concepts)
  • American Rhetoric: Online Speech Bank and Resources (Extensive database of speeches with rhetorical analysis tools)
