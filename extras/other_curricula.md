# Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue Rhetoric independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

- [Silva Rhetoricae: The Forest of Rhetoric](https://rhetoric.byu.edu/) (Comprehensive online reference for classical rhetoric terms and concepts)
- [American Rhetoric: Online Speech Bank and Resources](https://www.americanrhetoric.com/) (Extensive database of speeches with rhetorical analysis tools)
